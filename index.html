A voxel demo of the Cosmic Conquest universe

COSMICCONQUEST

The mechanics are the same: place units, hold the lane, do not lose your lives. The geometry is new: every unit is rebuilt as a 3D voxel rig in the faction palette of its power, on a floating arena in deep space. The roster of 20 units across 4 factions is the working roster from js/factions.js — translated, not invented.
